# City founding dates

Used to suggest the first player: the player holding the City card with the earliest founding date goes first (rulebook, Begin Play). Negative years are BC.

- Card: the date printed on the City card, read from the official rulebook illustrations (https://images-cdn.zmangames.com/us-east-1/filer_public/c3/62/c362beb7-bb07-4834-92f9-693de3f4eda5/zm7120_pandemic_iberia_rules.pdf).
- Researched: the card is not pictured in the rulebook, so the date comes from the source listed. Where only a century is known, its midpoint is used. These may differ from the printed cards; correct them in `FOUNDED` in `src/ui.js`.
